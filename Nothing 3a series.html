Key Highlights:

  • Display:

    Both Nothing Phone 3a and 3a Pro feature a 6.77-inch AMOLED display with 120Hz refresh rate, vibrant colors, HDR10+ support, and up to 1300 nits brightness, delivering an ultra-smooth and sharp visual experience perfect for gaming and media.

  • Performance:

    Powered by the Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 / Gen 3 chipset, both models offer reliable everyday performance, good multitasking, and moderate gaming capabilities, with smooth OxygenOS-like Nothing OS on top.

  • Camera System:

    Phone 3a: Dual 50MP cameras (main + ultrawide) offer sharp photos and solid 4K video. Phone 3a Pro: Adds a 3x periscope telephoto lens for excellent zoom shots, making it a better choice for camera lovers.

  • Battery and Charging:

    Both models pack a 5000mAh battery with 45W fast charging, ensuring long battery life and full charging in around 1 hour — no wireless charging though.

  • Build and Design:

    Carrying forward Nothing’s iconic transparent back with Glyph Interface, IP64 splash resistance, Gorilla Glass 5 front, and a new sleeker industrial design — bold and futuristic.

  • Software Support:

    Both models run Nothing OS 2.5.5 on Android 14 out of the box, with a promise of 3 years of major Android updates and 4 years of security patches, providing a clean, near-stock Android experience.
