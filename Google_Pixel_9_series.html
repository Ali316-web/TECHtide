Key Highlights:

  • Display:

    The Pro and Pro XL upgrade to LTPO Super Actua panels for smoother, more power-efficient scrolling, while the Pro XL offers the largest screen at 6.8 inches.

  • Performance:

    All three models share the same Google Tensor G4 chipset, with the Pro models getting extra RAM to handle Google's on-device AI features more smoothly.

  • Camera Capabilities:

    Both Pro models add a 5x telephoto lens and a significantly upgraded 42MP selfie camera, while the standard Pixel 9 sticks to a capable dual-camera setup.

  • Battery Life:

    The Pixel 9 Pro XL's larger 5,060mAh battery gives it the longest endurance in the lineup, with all models supporting fast wired and wireless charging.

  • Build Quality:

    The Pro and Pro XL step up to titanium frames for a more premium, durable feel compared to the aluminum frame on the standard Pixel 9.

  • Software Support:

    Google promises 7 years of OS, security, and Feature Drop updates across the entire Pixel 9 series, among the longest support commitments in the industry.
