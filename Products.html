Key Highlights:

  • Display:

    All models feature Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X displays with a 120Hz refresh rate, ensuring smooth visuals. The S25 Ultra offers the largest screen at 6.9 inches.

    Powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset, the series delivers robust performance across all models.

    The S25 Ultra stands out with its 200 MP main camera and versatile lens setup, catering to photography enthusiasts. The S25 and S25+ share a similar triple-camera configuration suitable for everyday photography.

  • Software Support:

    Samsung guarantees seven years of OS and security updates for the S25 series, ensuring longevity and access to the latest features.
