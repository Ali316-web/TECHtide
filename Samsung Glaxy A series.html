Key Highlights:

  • Display:

    All Galaxy A series models feature a 6.7-inch FHD+ Super AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and up to 1300 nits brightness (A56 & A36), delivering ultra-smooth visuals, vivid colors, and excellent outdoor visibility.

  • Performance:

    A56 is powered by the Exynos 1580, A36 by Snapdragon 6 Gen 3, A26 by Exynos 1380, ensuring smooth multitasking, casual gaming, and efficient daily performance across the lineup.

  • Camera System:

    All models feature a sharp 50MP main camera, with the A56 and A36 offering extra ultra-wide and macro lenses for versatile photography, while the A26 focuses on essential high-quality shots.

  • Battery and Charging:

    Each phone houses a large 5000mAh battery, with 45W fast charging on A56 and A36, and 25W charging on A26 — easily lasting a full day and recharging quickly when needed.

    All devices sport a sleek, symmetrical design with IP67 water and dust resistance, while the A56 feels more premium with a Gorilla Glass Victus+ front and aluminum frame, compared to the plastic frames on A36 and A26.

  • Software Support:

    Launching with Android 15 and One UI 7, the entire Galaxy A lineup promises 6 years of software and security updates, ensuring long-term reliability and access to the latest features.
