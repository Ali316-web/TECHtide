Key Highlights:

  • Display:

    All three models feature high refresh rate AMOLED panels, with the X200 Ultra offering the sharpest WQHD+ resolution and the brightest 4,500 nit peak brightness of the lineup.

  • Performance:

    The X200 and X200 Pro run on MediaTek's Dimensity 9400 chipset, while the X200 Ultra steps up to the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite for top-tier gaming and multitasking performance.

  • Camera System:

    Co-engineered with Zeiss, the X200 Pro and X200 Ultra pack a 200 MP periscope telephoto lens for exceptional zoom, while the standard X200 offers a versatile triple 50 MP setup for everyday shooting.

  • Battery and Charging:

    Every model in the series carries a large battery with fast 90W wired charging, while the Pro and Ultra add wireless charging support on top.

  • Build and Design:

    The entire lineup carries IP68 and IP69 dust and water resistance, giving all three phones durability that matches their premium glass-and-metal construction.

  • Software Support:

    Vivo ships the series with Android 15 and OriginOS/FuntouchOS on top, backed by four major OS updates and five years of security patches.
